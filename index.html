Process

How It Works

Our streamlined process makes e-waste disposal simple and hassle-free.

1

Schedule a Pickup

Book a collection online, via phone, or through our mobile app. Choose a date and time that works for you.

2

We Collect

Our trained team arrives at your location, carefully collects your e-waste, and provides a collection receipt.

3

Sorting & Processing

Items are sorted at our facility. Usable components are refurbished while others are prepared for recycling.

4

Responsible Recycling

Materials are processed using eco-friendly methods. Metals, plastics, and rare earth elements are recovered.

5

Certificate & Report

Receive a detailed recycling certificate and environmental impact report for your records.
